# Direct AI live-price paper account v1
Authorization: Ken requested "直接好似佢咁做實時模擬交易" on 2026-10-23. This is a separate prospective AI-discretionary paper account within the existing project, NOT the frozen ETH three-arm experiment and not proof of its gates. Keep old accounts, results and paused automation unchanged.
Virtual initial cash 10000 USDT. Spot long-only BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT/SOLUSDT; public Binance market data only. No order/account/wallet APIs, keys, leverage, paid services or real money.
Actual assistant decides buy/sell/hold using fresh timestamped market evidence and previous journal. Rules are risk constraints, not a coded AI substitute. No forced trades. Each new position costs at most 10% of pretrade NAV, aggregate exposure <=30%, no adding to an existing position. Fee 0.1% and adverse slippage 0.05% each side. Entry decision specifies stop at -3%, target +6%, expiry 24h. These are experimental limits, not validated optimal parameters.
Hourly polling only; never claim continuous/second-level monitoring or guaranteed stop prices. On each run, service exits first using CURRENT observable bid after a saved exit decision. Stops/targets are checked at polling time only; never infer intrahour fills. Due exits remain pending on outage. 3% HKT daily NAV drawdown blocks buys for that day; exits remain allowed.
SOT: direct_ai/runtime.json contains cash, positions, cumulative fees, realized PnL, timestamped marks, high-water NAV/drawdown, decisions and fills. Preserve history and use GitHub blob-SHA compare-and-swap, read back each write. Any conflict requires re-read/recompute. Never reset cash or backfill trades.
Sequence: persist raw evidence + AI decision before requesting a NEW ticker for fill; fill source closeTime must exceed persisted decision time. Max quote age 60s and max decision-to-fill delay 10min. Buy at ask*(1+.0005), sell at bid*(1-.0005), apply fee .001. Require quoted top-level quantity sufficient; otherwise skip. Retried decision IDs cannot double-fill. Fills are simulations, not real exchange executions.
Record hold decisions and missing data honestly. Fetch latest 48 hourly candles, discard unclosed candles, check continuity. News absent means unknown, never no-news. Current price is not proof of edge.
After each run persist NAV/positions and rationale; report fills and meaningful blockers, plus a concise daily summary on first run after 20:00 HKT. Report net return, realized/unrealized PnL, fees, exposure, observed drawdown and latency; AI subscription cost is excluded/unknown. Review hypotheses, preserve losses and no-trades. Changes to strategy are versioned prospectively.
Runner: engine.py provides deterministic accounting CLI; assistant supplies the actual decision. No exchange credentials or network execution in engine.
