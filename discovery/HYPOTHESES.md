## What the data actually suggested
These are event averages, not claimed portfolio returns. Each event uses genuine delayed minute entry/exit, base costs, and greedy horizon-spacing. Some backtest entries may differ because the account does not exit and re-enter at the same minute; do not extrapolate event means as an account return.

1. **Short-horizon costs are a barrier.** The average across exploratory cells' mean 4-hour gross returns was about 0.031%, versus about -0.269% after modeled round-trip costs. This is a summary across inspected cells, NOT an unconditional market return or an inferential estimate. Many apparently positive small price changes cannot cover fees/slippage.
2. **D1: volume-confirmed sell-off may have a 3-day rebound.** Four-hour standardized shock <= -2 and latest-hour quote volume >= twice the strictly earlier 7-day hourly median: 72-hour average net event return BTC +0.3509% (141 nonoverlap events), ETH +0.6863% (147). Corresponding 24-hour averages were negative. Both coins have weak/negative 2022 averages. Mechanism is a hypothesis about temporary forced selling, NOT measured liquidations or psychological panic. No liquidation data is claimed.
3. **D2: volume-confirmed 7-day breakout may persist.** Hourly close above the maximum high of the strictly previous 168 complete hours, latest hourly quote volume >=2x earlier 7-day hourly median, hold 72h. Training average net event return BTC +0.5970% (79 events), ETH +1.6378% (84); ETH's three annual averages positive, BTC 2021 -0.0798%. BTC's aggregate excess vs same-month unconditional events is slightly negative; we are not concealing that caveat. Hypothesis: volume distinguishes a demand-driven move from a thin breakout. It is unproven.
4. **D3: volatility expansion may identify a useful 24h exposure window.** 24-hour log-return standard deviation divided by strictly earlier 168-hour deviation is high. Training Q80 was 1.2007616 BTC / 1.1900845 ETH; freeze the common rounded threshold 1.2, not a later optimized cut. Q5 average net 24h event return was +0.1653% BTC (316 events) / +0.1881% ETH (318); both had negative 2022 absolute averages but positive same-month excess in each year. Mechanism could be a volatility-regime effect, not necessarily positive directional forecasting.
5. **D4: cross-asset asymmetry, not a symmetric pairs trade.** In ETH, when its 24h return lags BTC by at least 2 percentage points, BTC rose at least 2% over 24h, and ETH's 30-day return is positive, next-24h mean net event return was +1.0349% (82 events). Annual means +1.5001%, +1.3548%, +0.1251%; only seven events in 2022, so evidence is fragile. The mirrored BTC-lags-ETH state had -0.4599% mean (149 events). Freeze ETH as the hypothesized asset; run the BTC mirror as a predeclared negative control, not as a post-results rejection. Do not claim a market-neutral hedge; this is long-only spot exposure.
